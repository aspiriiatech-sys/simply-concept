What Will Be Covered in the Program

The program is designed to strengthen current foundations and prepare students for the next academic year. The exact pace and focus may vary slightly based on batch level and student readiness.

Class 5–6

Focus: Build strong basic maths understanding and problem-solving confidence.

Topics covered may include:

  • Number sense and basic operations
  • Fractions and decimals
  • Measurement and time
  • Basic geometry
  • Word problems
  • Patterns and logical puzzles

Class 7–8

Focus: Strengthen middle-school maths foundations and improve application skills.

Topics covered may include:

  • Fractions, decimals, and percentages
  • Ratio and proportion
  • Integers and basic algebra
  • Linear equations
  • Mensuration basics
  • Logical reasoning and analytical puzzles

Class 9

Focus: Strengthen concepts essential for higher-level school maths.

Topics covered may include:

  • Algebra foundation
  • Linear equations
  • Coordinate geometry basics
  • Geometry and theorem understanding
  • Surface area and volume basics
  • Analytical thinking and problem-solving practice

Class 10

Focus: Improve concept clarity and confidence for important school-level maths topics.

Topics covered may include:

  • Algebra revision and strengthening
  • Trigonometry basics
  • Coordinate geometry
  • Geometry confidence building
  • Application-based problem solving
  • Structured reasoning practice

Note: Final content and pace may slightly vary based on the student’s class level, batch, and learning readiness.
